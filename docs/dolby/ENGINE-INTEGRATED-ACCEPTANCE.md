## Evidence discipline

- Codec `canPlayType()`, HLS `CODECS`, supported native HLS or a high-dynamic-range media query are **capability hints**, not Dolby Vision/Atmos output proof.
- RPU unit hashes verify *ordering and boundaries* when present. They do not prove a browser applied those units to decoded pixels, and the HLS semantic stage stays `not-established`.
- Offline FFprobe decoded-frame PTS matching proves a container-level association, not the frame sequence actually rendered by WebKit.
- The synthetic AC-3/E-AC-3 CI audio is generated by FFmpeg and does not qualify as real Dolby media acceptance.
- The experimental OpenJOC decoder is not installed as an automatic replacement for the normal audio output, nor is its PCM speaker output an Atmos certification.
- Do not merge or deploy to production before explicit authorization and the applicable release gates. An independent Preview workflow running does not imply successful deployment.
